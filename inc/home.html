Our Services

Power to Sheds & Modular Homes

Building a shed, a granny flat or putting in a modular home? We run underground cabling from your main switchboard, fit a sub-board, and add the lights and power points you need, so it's ready to use from day one and fully above board.

Switchboard Upgrades & Surge Protection

Old ceramic fuses and missing safety switches are a common find in older Fraser Coast homes. We upgrade switchboards with modern circuit breakers and RCD safety switches, and can add surge protection to help guard your TV, computers and appliances from power spikes.

Power Points & Switches

Not enough outlets, or power boards piled up behind the TV? We add new power points inside and out, including USB outlets and weatherproof outdoor points, and replace worn or broken switches so everything works the way it should.

Lighting & Ceiling Fans

From LED downlights and pendant lights to outdoor wall lights and sensor lights, we install and upgrade lighting right through your home. We also fit new ceiling fans, or swap out the old wobbly ones, to keep things cool through a Queensland summer.

Split System Air Conditioning

We install split system air conditioners in bedrooms, living areas and granny flats, including the dedicated circuit, isolator and a sensible spot for the outdoor unit. Already bought a unit? We can install it, or help you choose the right size for the room.

EV Charger Installation

Bought an electric vehicle? We install home EV chargers in your garage or carport, check your switchboard can handle the extra load, and run a dedicated circuit so you can charge overnight safely and much faster than a standard power point.

Smoke Alarm Upgrades

Queensland law requires every home to have interconnected photoelectric smoke alarms by 1 January 2027. We install interconnected alarms in the right locations, so when one goes off they all go off, and your home meets the rules well before the deadline.

Fault Finding & Repairs

Safety switch keeps tripping? Power out to half the house, or a light that flickers no matter what you do? We track down the cause with proper testing, explain what we've found in plain English, and get it sorted so it doesn't come back.

Not sure which one you need? Give us a ring and we'll talk it through.
